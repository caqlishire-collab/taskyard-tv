Taskyard

Privacy policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

Taskyard keeps your tasks on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Taskyard stores

Your tasks, lists, tags and settings are saved in Taskyard’s private storage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Wall TV board (optional)

If you turn on Settings, Wall TV, Publish to wall TV, Taskyard copies the open tasks from the lists you mark “Show on TV” to Apple’s iCloud, so a TV can show them. Only those lists are copied, and only while the setting is on. Anyone who has the board’s 8 character code can view that board, so share the code only with people who should see it. Turn the setting off to stop updating the board.

Sharing with people (optional)

If you invite people from People, Invite people, Taskyard copies your lists and tasks to your own private iCloud and shares them only with the people you invite, using Apple’s iCloud sharing. Each task records the name of who created it and who finished it, using the name you enter in People. You can remove people or stop sharing at any time from the same screen. The developer cannot see your shared tasks.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Taskyard schedules reminders for your tasks on your device. Your device’s operating system creates and delivers them.

What Taskyard collects

Only what you choose to show on a wall TV: if Wall TV publishing is on, the open tasks in the lists you marked are stored in Taskyard’s iCloud database so the TV can show them. This is not linked to your identity and is used only to run that feature. Taskyard does not collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts or identifiers, and does not track you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. To remove a wall TV board, turn off publishing; the board stops updating and shows nothing new.
