Common questions

How do I add a task quickly?

Type in the bar at the bottom, for example “Pay rent tomorrow 9am #home !high”. Taskyard picks up the date, time, tag and priority.

Why did Taskyard warn me about a double booking?

The time overlaps another open task. Choose “Move to” the next free time, “Book anyway”, or “Cancel”. You can block overlaps entirely in Settings.

How do reminders work?

Timed tasks remind you 1 hour before they start, and all day tasks at 9:00 AM. Change this in Settings, Reminders, or turn off a single task’s reminder in its details.

How do I show my schedule on a TV?

Mark lists with “Show on TV”, turn on Settings, Wall TV, then open this site in the TV’s browser and enter the board code.
